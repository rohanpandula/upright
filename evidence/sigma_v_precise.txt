METHOD
------
For each image the known world verticals are projected through the true camera,
the Auto homography (UprightTransform_1, row-major, normalised coords) is applied
in pixel space, and the signed vertical tilt is measured before and after:

    init   = mean signed tilt of the visible verticals from image vertical (deg)
    resid  = the same after warping
    frac   = 1 - resid/init  ==  corrected angle / true angle

Fraction is therefore measured geometrically, not inferred from the focal
estimate.  Model fitted by robust (soft_l1) least squares:

    frac(theta) = lam/(lam+1),  lam = L0 * exp(-theta^2 / (2 sigma^2))

95% CI is a cluster bootstrap that resamples the 20 ANGLES (2000 replicates),
which is the honest error bar for a curve that is bimodal image-to-image.
Robustness: fitting per-angle medians gives sigma 21.90, per-angle means 21.78,
leave-one-angle-out range 19.1..23.8 -- all consistent with 22.3.

ANGLE UNITS -- why three sigmas
-------------------------------
The walls are at z=25..80 with |u|/f ~ 0.36, so at pitch theta the verticals in
frame are only ~0.15*theta off vertical and the extreme-vertical convergence is
~0.6*theta.  The solver's internal angle estimate lives on the visible-keystone
scale, not on the camera-pitch scale.  Consequences:

  * 22.26 deg is sigma per degree of camera pitch (the task's definition);
  * 13.33 deg is sigma per degree of visible keystone -- the apples-to-apples
    comparison with an internal binary literal, and it brackets 15.00 deg;
  * 2.79 deg is sigma per degree of the solver's own tilt estimate.

The literal's curve, if it were the shipped one, would fall far too fast at the
top end.  With the fitted L0 held fixed and sigma forced to 15.00 deg:

    theta        fitted frac     literal-sigma frac     observed mean
      15            0.678              0.615               ~0.80 (14-16 deg)
      30            0.516              0.263               0.552
      40            0.344              0.070               0.378

So the shipped curve sits ~2x above the literal prediction at 30 deg and ~5x at 40 deg, and lam
falls to L0/2 at 17.7 deg for the literal versus 26.2 deg for the measurement.  The shipped constant is not pi/12.


CAVEATS
-------
1. Bimodality.  Above ~30 deg the solver sometimes clamps its focal estimate at
   the 40 mm ceiling and returns a near-identity transform (frac ~0.03), and
   sometimes keeps a free estimate (~25 mm) and corrects ~0.75.  The branch is
   random across seeds (e.g. 38 deg: 0.028 and 0.780).  24/80 images (30%) had a
   degenerate focal estimate (20 clamped at 40 mm, 4 left at the 35 mm default),
   and all 4 total abstentions were at 38-40 deg.  This is why the residual is
   large and the CI wide; a smooth curve through the branch mixture is still the
   right estimand for an adaptive weight.
2. Pure pitch keeps verticals vertical in a pinhole model only at the principal
   point; off-centre verticals tilt by |u|/f * theta, which is what is measured.
3. The same sweep at 2 deg under-corrects (0.277), so the fitted asymptote 0.725
   is approached only over 6-28 deg -- there is a low-angle shoulder the two-
   parameter model does not represent.
4. UprightTransform_N is the cached Auto slot; each image was reset to
   PerspectiveUpright=0 before Auto so no stale slot could be read.
