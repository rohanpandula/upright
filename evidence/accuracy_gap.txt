Changes to the objective
------------------------
[PAPER structure / clean-room derivation] E_pic transforms the OBSERVED support
line with H^-T, not a fictional line through the fitted VP and segment midpoint.
[PROVEN weights] Use mean-normalised source-pixel lengths (detector.txt 3.4),
normalised across all source segments, rather than treating every chain equally.
[PAPER geometry] Convert directions back to pixels before angle normalisation;
normalised x/y have different scales on a non-square image.
[MEASURED robust choice] Cauchy loss rho(r)=s^2*log(1+(r/s)^2), with
s=4/(maxdim-1); sum-of-squares residual is sign(r)*sqrt(rho(r)). The scale is
from the resolution tolerance in detector.txt 4.2; Cauchy itself is OUR choice,
not recovered Adobe code or a claim about its energy. It suppresses residual
misassignments without changing the camera family or damping semantics.
[PAPER fork domain] The previous _junctions invented 120 forks by intersecting
infinite pairs of lines anywhere in the frame. A two-line crossing is not a
three-ray fork. Its soft angle-to-90 penalty is removed; no true fork detector
exists here. This is not a recovered log barrier. E_img also remains unported.
The existing per-mode scalar energy weights (1,4,30 etc.) are inherited choices,
not newly fitted/claimed Adobe constants. The f1/f0 term regulates DESTINATION
focal; source f0 remains fixed, exactly as before.

Detector implementation choices
-------------------------------
See detector_corrections.txt for the recovered NFA and corrected call constants.
The existing blur sigma=1.2, 85th magnitude percentile and 1200px working policy
are retained, not measured Adobe literals. Quadratic interpolation of gradient
maxima uses the mathematical half-step formula, clipped to half a pixel. This
removes staircase bias in short raster supports. TLS refinement splits bent
supports until within the recovered 1px distance, bounded by 99 passes. The
connected-support/TLS choices are re-derived and explicitly not pixel parity.
There is no fixed 8px minimum, density gate, or invented minimum VP-inlier count
in detector validation. Strict length-bin support and NFA decide significance.

Corrections to prior conclusions
-------------------------------
1. 01_input.png is NOT a hard real photograph. demo_synthetic.py generates it,
   with F=1400px, pose=(-0.38,0.10,0.04). The old status number used f0=1200px.
   On the exact ORIGINAL inliers and wrong 1200px focal, preserve this diagnostic:
     old:              0.436578 / 0.223640
     energy only:      0.205170 / 0.327228
     new end-to-end:   0.189513 / 0.270213
   Horizontal gets worse under that protocol. Do not report universal improvement
   or silently substitute a different focal to claim a win. With the known correct
   focal, independent ground-truth results improve both families (table above).
2. Fixing K0 with a wrong focal can make source Manhattan directions nonorthogonal.
   A rotation cannot make two nonorthogonal rays into the orthogonal output axes
   at infinity; free destination focal/centre does not remove that obstruction.
   This is not solely a tunable energy-prior problem. K0 is unchanged by this task.
3. The report's Adobe numbers do NOT say automatic Full reaches zero on all these
   scenes: e.g. section 3.4 gives 0.73/0.36 convergence, section 3.6 gives about
   0.98/0.23 on B. Guided is the measured exactly-zero mode. Do not compare these
   axis scores numerically to the report's different convergence metric.
4. Detector notes have byte-width/branch/rounding/NFA errors, documented separately
   in detector_corrections.txt. Exact detector equivalence cannot honestly be
   claimed from those notes alone. Nearest bins are retained to honour the user's
   explicit formula and original tests; float1.0/6px follow the actual instructions.
