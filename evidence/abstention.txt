ABSTENTION — RESOLVED: it is not a veto, it is "nothing to correct"

LAYER SPLIT (the test that mattered)
Abstention can live at the input (detector), solver, or output (gate) layer.
Discriminator: a real UprightFocalLength35mm means the detector AND estimator ran;
the default 35 means they never got that far.

PROBE: 17 degradations of one 18deg-pitch / 6deg-yaw scene, four axes
  noise  sigma 4/12/28/55
  blur   radius 2/4/7/11 px
  contrast 45/25/12/5 %
  crop   keep 55/35/22/12 % width
  + untouched control

RESULT: ZERO abstentions. Every image produced a real focal estimate (31.6-40.8 mm)
and a strong correction. Auto/Full magnitude ratio stayed 0.58-0.90 throughout --
Auto never collapsed relative to Full, even at 12% width.
=> noise, blur, contrast and crop are NOT the trigger, and it is not an output gate.

CORPUS TEST (the decisive one)
For every photo with both slots stored, compare Full's own transform magnitude:
  abstainers (Auto identity)  n=1422 : Full |H-I| median 0.0000, p90 0.0000
  corrected                   n=13200: Full |H-I| median 0.1657, p90 0.6802
  Full ALSO identity in 100.0% of abstainers, vs 9.7% of corrected.

Full's magnitude distribution is a smooth continuum, not a step:
  p10 0.0004 | p25 0.0261 | p50 0.1363 | p75 0.3623 | p90 0.6434

CONCLUSION
The solver decides ONCE per photo, not per mode. When the required correction is
negligible it emits identity into every slot, and all six slots inherit it. The
9.8-14% "abstainers" are not hard photos being given up on -- they are photos that
are already upright. There is no confidence gate, no content test and no metadata
signal because there is nothing to gate: abstention is the objective function
correctly returning "no work to do".

The residual band where Full does ~0.0045 and Auto rounds to identity is just the
floor of the same continuum.

PORTING IMPLICATION: do not implement an abstention heuristic. Emit identity when
the solved correction is below noise, and let the magnitude do the work. The
14% figure is a property of the photo population, not of a hidden rule.
