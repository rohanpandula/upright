RED-TEAM: §3.1 "H = s·K1·R·K0⁻¹, two different intrinsics, proven exactly"
=====================================================================
Verdict: the *residual* is real and reproduces. The *interpretation* is
substantially overstated. The test cannot tell "two cameras" from "one
camera with translation" (or any realistic homography), and "K0 is the
stored values" is false for the exact fit.

Independent reimplementation: redteam/core.py + redteam/analyze2.py.
Data: live catalog, mode=ro, Adobe_imageDevelopSettings, UprightTransform_*.
n=400 random photos, slot 2 (Full). All coordinates in pixels.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE RESIDUAL CLAIM REPRODUCES (confirm)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Reimplementation from the description alone, synthetic corpus first:
  median 1.28e-16, max 1.129e-09  (report: 5.65e-16 / 1.13e-09)
Same max to 3 digits. Real corpus, slot 2, n=400:
  median 2.163e-16, p90 6.207e-16, max 1.346e-09, 99.0% < 1e-9
So the arithmetic is correct and the report is not fabricating numbers.
It holds across slots (median residual, 99% band):

  slot 2 Full (n=400)   2.16e-16   99.0% <1e-9
  slot 1 Auto (n=200)   2.22e-16   94.5% <1e-9
  slot 4 Vertical (n=200) 2.05e-16 98.5% <1e-9

But note where the 1e-9 tail comes from: it is the *Level* slot and
near-level/affine transforms, not random noise. Slot 3 alone: median
5.8e-10, max 1.08e-9. On genuinely projective transforms the floor is
~1e-16. The published "max 1.13e-09" is a Level artifact.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE OVER-PARAMETERISATION CONFOUND (task 2) — single-K does NOT
   recover, but the framing "10 vs 8" is miscounted
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The claim's model has 10 unknowns, but 6 of them are intrinsic and only 5
orthogonality equations are independent (trace(M - s²I) ≡ 0). 6 > 5, so
an exact solution is generic: there is a 1-parameter family, and the
residual Jacobian has exactly one zero singular value at every fitted
solution tested. f0 can be driven over at least 0.25×–4× the stored f35
while c0 compensates (see §4). The model is not literally "any H" — pure
affine transforms fail — but every realistic perspective warp passes
(§3).

"Single-K with a free principal point" is not 10 unknowns. It is
f(1) + c(2) + R(3) + s(1) = 7. Results (n=400, median residual):

  model                                    median    p90      pass<1e-9
  two-K, f0,f1,c0,c1 free (report)         2.16e-16  6.2e-16  99.0%
  single-K, f + shared free c (3 intr.)    1.056e-01 5.5e-01   5.8%
  single-K, general K: fx,fy,skew,c (5)    9.878e-02 4.7e-01   5.8%
  shared f + both centres free (5 intr.)   1.030e-01 6.3e-01  10.8% (n=120)
  conventional shared f, fixed centre      1.060e-01 6.5e-01   8.3%
  single-K + output anisotropy (6 intr.)   5.936e-02 3.1e-01  17.7%

The ~6-18% "passes" are the identity/abstention photos, where everything
fits trivially. So freeing the principal point does not rescue single-K;
neither does freeing aspect and skew. The two-focal model is genuinely
distinguished from every single-focal model tested. That part survives.
Same on other slots: single-K + free pp gives 4.8e-2 median on Auto and
5.3e-2 on Vertical (both 6% pass), against 2.2e-16 / 2.1e-16 for the
two-focal model.

Caveat on §3.1's "32%": on the real corpus the conventional model is
10.6% off, not 32%. 32% is a synthetic-scene figure.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. IS 5.65e-16 SUSPICIOUS? (task 3) — yes, largely
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The condition is satisfiable by construction once both K's are free.
Set P = K0K0ᵀ, Q = K1K1ᵀ. The condition is H P Hᵀ = s² Q. Pick any SPD P;
Q = H P Hᵀ/s² is SPD automatically; factor it (upper Cholesky) to get K1.
There is no residual equation left. That construction uses a general
upper-triangular K; the shipped parameterisation is isotropic, which is
6 intrinsic parameters against 5 equations, so a solution is still
generic. Machine-precision agreement is therefore the expected outcome,
not a discovery.

Decisive control — homographies from models that have nothing to do with
the shipped solver:

  input                                      median residual
  10 random "realistic perspective warps"    1.5e-16  (10/10 < 1e-9)
  single camera, rotation + translation     1.5e-16 - 1.9e-16 (all t)
     (K(R+t nᵀ)K⁻¹, |t| = 0 … 0.3)
  pure translation homography                0.0
  pure affine (random 2x2 + translation)     0.13 - 0.74  (0/5)
  pure shear / anisotropic scale             0.28 / 0.59
  random projective (h20,h21 ~ 1)            ~0.7 - 1.0

The rotation+translation row matters most. A single camera with a
translation is the ordinary alternative to "two cameras, rotation only",
and the two-focal model absorbs it exactly at every translation magnitude
tested. So the exactness test does not even establish that the shipped
transform is translation-free, let alone that it came from two cameras.

A shear needs only h20 ≥ 1e-5 added before it fits at 1e-16. Every real
photo homography is of the form K·A·K⁻¹ with A arbitrary, and that class
passes. So the orthogonality test is a consistency check that almost any
plausible photo homography passes. It does not identify the algorithm.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE REAL DAMAGE: the decomposition is not identifiable (task 3/5)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The report says "2-parameter family" and "the ambiguity is resolved in
practice — the stored values are the source camera". Both are wrong.

(a) The family is 1-dimensional, not 2. Fixing f0 and letting c0 float
    fits exactly; fixing c0 (either coordinate) and letting f0 float does
    not (residual 1e-3 to 1e-1). f0 and c0 slide together, so neither is
    measured.
(b) f0 is free over a wide range. Pinning f0 to a multiple of the stored
    f35 and fitting the rest (n=400):

      f0 pinned at      median residual   pass<1e-9
      0.25 × f35        2.457e-16         99.0%
      0.5  × f35        3.082e-16         99.0%
      1.0  × f35        4.281e-16         99.0%
      2.0  × f35        4.953e-16         86.0%
      4.0  × f35        8.158e-16         62.0%

    A factor-4 range of source focal is exact. f0 is not measured. On the
    Auto slot (n=200) even 0.25× to 4× all pass at 94.5% — identical to
    the unconstrained fit, i.e. the pinned f0 is completely inert there.
    Vertical (n=200): 0.25×–2× pass at 98.5%, 4× at 90.5%.
(c) Same H, three different inits, all 1e-16:
      img 3005: f0 = 4923 / 12565 / 8785 ; c0 x = 1702 / 3437 / 2380
      img 3090: f0 = 10746 / 20616 / 13986 ; c0 x = -4128 / 617 / -2963
    Recovered c1 lands outside the image in 13% of cases. c0 never does
    only because the optimiser is initialised at the stored value.
(d) K0 fixed to the *stored* f35 and stored centre, fitting only f1,c1
    (n=400): median 5.1e-7, p90 1.5e-2, max 8.6e-2, exact for 14.7%
    (and 23 of the 59 passes are identity photos). So the stored pair is
    close but not exact. Pin f0 at f35 and free only c0: exact, but c0
    moves away from the stored centre by up to 0.68 × image width
    (img 3090: stored x 4298 → fitted −1664). K1 fixed to stored instead:
    median 8.5e-2 (n=15). The stored focal is roughly right; the stored
    centre is not K0's centre.

Consequence: "K0 is directly readable from the settings" and "take f0 and
c0 from the stored values and solve only f1,c1,R,s" describe a *different,
inexact* model (5e-7 to 1e-2 residual, exact for 15%), not the one that
reaches 1e-16. The exact fit only works because c0 is free, and f0 can
additionally slide over 0.25–4×.

5. DEGRADATION (task 4)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  subset                     n     median      max
  all slot 2                 400   2.16e-16    1.35e-9
  |roll| > 20°                4    8.59e-11    2.96e-10
  |pitch| > 35°               6    1.86e-16    4.62e-16
  abstention (Full identity)  -    0.0         0.0
  Level slot (slot 3)         15   5.77e-10    1.08e-9
  Auto slot (slot 1), n=200   200  2.22e-16    4.5e-3

Extreme pitch and abstention are clean. Strong roll degrades by ~6 orders
(still 1e-10, far below the 1e-1 single-K floor). Level/affine is the
weak spot at 1e-9. Auto degrades badly on near-identity transforms: all 8
of 200 failures have |H-I| between 1e-3 and 1.4e-2, i.e. a ~10% relative
error on tiny corrections. No subset breaks the model outright.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT SURVIVES, AND WHAT TO SOFTEN (task 5)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Strongest surviving claim:
  "Adobe's stored transforms are consistent, to float noise, with a
   rotation-only homography carrying two independently free focal
   lengths. Within the rotation-only family they are inconsistent (median
   ~0.10) with every single-focal model tested, including one with a free
   principal point, free aspect and skew. Two focal lengths are therefore
   a real algebraic requirement of the output — but not proof of two
   cameras, because a single camera with translation also fits exactly."

Soften or cut:
  1. "proven exactly" / "not a guess — the shipped algebra, recovered to
     15 digits" → *consistent with*, not identified. Any realistic warp
     passes (10/10). The test is necessary, not sufficient.
  2. "distinguished ... by an orthogonality test" → it is distinguished
     from K·R·K⁻¹ and from free-principal-point single-K, but not from a
     general homography.
  3. "2-parameter family" → 1-parameter family. Say the intrinsics are
     not identifiable.
  4. "K0 is directly readable from the settings", "the ambiguity
     collapses in practice" → false for the exact model. Stored K0 gives
     5e-7 median and is exact for only 15% of photos; f0 is free over
     0.25-4×. Keep the stored-focal-as-K0 story only for the *inexact*
     3-parameter variant, and label it approximate.
  5. "median 0.32" for single-K → 0.106 on the real corpus; 0.32 is
     synthetic-only.
  6. Replace "two cameras" with "two focal lengths" until a camera can be
     identified. The recovered K1 has no independent evidence of being a
     real camera; its centre is often off-image.

The §3.1 equation is a good description of the output's algebraic shape.
It is not proof that the solver has two cameras, and the §3.5c corollary
is the part most in need of rewrite.
