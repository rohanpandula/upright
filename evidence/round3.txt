2. CAUSAL MECHANISM AND FIX
Reproduced before production edits. Ranked hypotheses: stale post-reassignment VP;
Round-1-style family selection; biased/ambiguous supports. The first is causal here.
estimate_vanishing_points fits TLS on disjoint initial supports, then reassigns/expands
inliers. It returned the newly expanded supports with the OLD subset-fitted VP.
On H09 every original seed 0..4 selects the same 112 lateral and 189 vertical segments.
Yet seed 0 lateral VP in normalized coordinates is (2.256882,-0.823849,1), versus
(2.304001,-0.863254,1) at seed 2. The family identity and observed objective supports
are unchanged. The depth support count varies 80..82. The horizon term consumes these
stale lateral/depth VPs, forcing a seed-dependent roll/pitch/yaw compromise.
This is not the Round 1 lateral/depth family swap and is not inherently ambiguous input.

Controlled interventions, same raster/supports/focal, seed 0, independent world V/H:

  only disable horizon             0.011648 / 0.004884

  final robust selected refit      0.012235/0.005261
Full seed 0..4 interventions: round3_probe.txt; reproducible against round3_original_port.py.

Production _base_solve now fixes the selected family identities, then refines the selected
vertical/lateral/depth VPs on their final observed supports. _refine_vp uses centered
isotropic coordinates and length-weighted Cauchy angular IRLS with the existing four-pixel
angular scale (20 iterations maximum). Calibration, initialization and the enabled horizon
term consume these refined VPs. The raw hypothesis remains only for selecting family identity.
No world truth enters selection, refinement, calibration, optimization or the gate.

Two rejected implementation trials explain why the fix is more than another TLS SVD:
  - TLS refitting every VP creates angular-outlier bias on A/C and moves weak intersections.
    round3_tls_trial.json retains every trial; 18 bad fixture/focal candidates, 2 falsely passed.
  - Robustly refitting every unused VP before lateral selection can promote a weak unused
    intersection at wrong focal. round3_all_vps_trial.json retains the trial: 30 bad fixture/
    focal candidates, 26 falsely passed. Final refinement happens AFTER identity selection.
These trials were rejected, not substituted for the final benchmark or hidden in means.

3. WHAT THE QUALITY GATE MEASURES
Old worst_scale is absolute detected-line median sine-angle / [4/(long_side-1)].
It permitted up to 3 scale units: 0.672107 deg at H09 and 0.573445 deg at 01_input.
That detects gross optimizer failures; it is not calibrated for a 0.1 deg systematic error.
A smaller limit on the same absolute median would confuse detector noise with axis bias.

01_input correction: the quoted ~0.13 deg is the absolute DETECTED-SUPPORT residual, not
the independent world vertical error. Before, correct-prior seed 0 has world V/H
0.013563/0.061668, support V median 0.134608, signed V median -0.022745.
Final correct-prior seed 0:
  world V/H=0.012257/0.033289; support abs medians=0.133458/0.024216
  signed medians=-0.024147/-0.007874; margin=0.050280/0.010295
  bias bounds=0.074427/0.018168; worst_scale=0.698202.
If 0.13 refers to another seed/oracle, it must be labeled separately; it is not the world
error for this supplied fixture. Frozen legacy detector-inlier scores remain separate.

The gate retains the old raw-scatter catastrophe check and ADDS an explicit systematic-bias
budget: orient each output line toward its intended positive axis, compute the source-length-
weighted signed pixel-angle median m and weighted MAD. n_eff=(sum lengths)^2/sum(lengths^2).
Require |m| + 3.72*MAD/sqrt(n_eff) <= 0.1 deg for EACH family, before mode damping.
3.72 approximates 2 x 1.253 x 1.483 for a median/MAD under independent Gaussian errors.
This is a transparent support-consistency policy, NOT a statistical confidence interval or
a universal world-error bound: edge fragments can correlate, scene axes can be mislabeled,
and systematically biased evidence can fool an internally consistent fit. The current
synthetic sample does not calibrate real-photo confidence. The .1 budget comes from the task;
the four-pixel scale and median-margin constants are implementation choices, not Adobe rules.

Both original bad H09 fits fail the NEW bias gate even if the fitter is not repaired:
  seed 0: bound V/H=0.127432/0.091544, scatter_pass=True, bias_pass=False.
  seed 1: bound V/H=0.117861/0.078081, scatter_pass=True, bias_pass=False.
They return ok=False, identity, reason="poor undamped family alignment" when replayed
through solve_ex. New tests also reject .11/.13 deg coherent bias across three aspects,
and distinguish symmetric support scatter from the same supports with .13 deg bias.
