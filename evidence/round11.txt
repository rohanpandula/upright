ROUND 11 — INPUT RENDER VS SUPPORT/FAMILY SELECTION — 2026-10-28
================================================================================================================
ANSWER: support/family selection is a demonstrated causal contributor. A preview-extraction path is NOT
supported by the paired data. Better rendered pixels alone do not make the normal pipeline match Adobe.
With exactly the existing RAW detections, oracle axis-family selection cuts Full median error 3.663819% ->
0.932318% on all 387 RAW files, including 26 oracle failures returned as identity. This 74.55% median reduction
is a diagnostic intervention using Adobe truth, NOT a deployable fix or proof that all remaining error is detection.
No production code, gate, threshold, damping, focal, framing or scale rule was changed. No preview path shipped.

Frozen Round 10 corrected cohort: 450 photos. RAW intervention cohort: all 387 DNG/ARW/NEF/RAF files;

Both modes, seed 0, same original catalog dimensions, 1200px detector, metadata focal, framing and scale ON.

5. WHY FULL CAN FAIL MORE SEVERELY — SUPPORT/FAMILY INTERVENTION
Use EXACTLY the cached Round 10 RAW detections, not new pixels or edges. Transform their endpoints by native
Full, label segments whose output pixel angle is within 1 degree of vertical/horizontal, require >=3 per family.
Replace only VP/family selection with those oracle-labelled families; keep robust VP refinement, source-focal
calibration, optimization, damping, quality, framing and scale. No extra line is synthesized. There is no depth
family in this two-family diagnostic, so a separate no-depth control isolates that possible confound.

Across the full 450-photo frozen baseline, Auto and Full share the same 322 underlying fitted poses. The fitted

projective horizon in 75/450 attempts versus Auto 3/450; another 128/450 in both modes lack sufficient structure.
Those invalid Full maps become identity at the public API. Auto attenuates pitch/yaw; that can contain damage
from a spurious family/pose. Full amplifies it. This explains a failure mechanism, not the exact external rates.
The quoted 40.7% /86.1% /25.8% win rates cannot be reproduced from the frozen current returned-H protocol:
450-file shipped outputs have Auto W/L/T=24/16/410, Full=32/8/410; decisive rates=60%/80%. Gate-bypassed valid
maps have Auto 117/319=36.68%, Full 110/247=44.53%. Cohort, gate, map validity, identity ties, framing and metric
must be matched before reusing a rate. Absolute Full error is larger in this snapshot; a Full<Auto win-rate
inversion itself is not reproduced here. No claim that the external evaluations were invalid is made.

LIMITS / WHAT IS AND IS NOT CAUSAL
The controlled oracle-family intervention demonstrates that changing which existing supports constrain the fit
causes a large error reduction without changing render pixels. It uses the target Adobe homography to label
axes, so it is an upper-bound diagnostic, not an independent deployable classifier or world-line truth. The
no-depth control rules out removal of that term as the explanation. It does not disentangle pixel-edge semantics,
VP clustering, lateral-family ranking and the focal changes induced by different selected supports. That is the
next implementation seam; a genuine image-derived selector still needs to be designed and validated.
Even the oracle is not exact: all-387 Full mean=2.435441%, median=.932318%, p90=5.195489%, max=86.858380%;
40 attempts exceed 5%, four exceed 20%, including fallback identities. Its 75 accepted Full maps include two >5%.
Residual camera-center/focal, mode response, lens, output policy and non-Manhattan ambiguity remain possible.
This is a personal catalog, availability biased, with related captures/copies; no confidence interval assumes
independent photos. The 40-file render/lens probes are exploratory. Adobe cache digests are current; no native
re-solve, catalog write, photo modification, export or external publish was performed.
