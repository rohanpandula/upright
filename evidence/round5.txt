ROUND 5 — AUTO OVER-CORRECTION — 2026-10-28
========================================================================================
RESULT: NOT FIXED. No production change was retained. The requested semantic targets
and B_strong raw <=25.38 were NOT achieved. This report records a failed fix attempt,
not an improvement claim. Changing the angle units alone is insufficient. Framing is
a large raw-metric limitation, but missing native branch behavior is a separate
damping limitation; the failure cannot honestly be attributed to framing alone.

1. AXIS AUDIT — CURRENT CODE, NOT AN ASSUMPTION
port/upright_port.py correction_fraction_vertical applies:
  lambda=2.64*exp(-keystone^2/(2*13.33deg^2)); fraction=lambda/(1+lambda).
The input is P95-P5 signed detected vertical-support directions in pixel angles.
It is evaluated BEFORE correction. It is not camera pitch, mean vertical tilt,
or corrected/residual pitch. _auto_pitch_scale then numerically converts requested
convergence removal to an Euler rx scale while yaw/roll are held at their schedules.
Thus the current 13.33deg/2.64 pair uses matching KEYSTONE units. Round 4 already
fixed the suspected pitch/keystone mismatch. Keeping 13.33 on camera pitch would
be an error, not the Round 5 fix. That negative control is measured below.
Horizontal falloff: exp(-ry^2/(2*11.64deg^2)), gated by initial camera rx using
1-exp(-rx^2/(2*22.10deg^2)); LAMBDA0_H=76.73. Roll scales: Auto=.928, Level=.87.
All constants remain unchanged. A matching camera-pitch experiment used 22.26deg
with 2.64, with the same physical-removal conversion and the same solver supports.

2. REPRODUCTION AND RANKED HYPOTHESES
1) Wrong angle/stage or response transfer: falsified as a units bug; tested matching
   camera-pitch response and direct Euler scaling separately.
2) Coupled yaw/pitch/roll cannot be represented by independent Euler damping:
   tested four-start bounded fits of all three damping scales and a horizon-roll
   diagnostic during investigation. No evidenced replacement roll model emerged.
3) Pooled Gaussian misses the low-angle shoulder and strong-angle branch mixture:
   tested interpolation of per-angle mean fractions from all solved dense-sweep
   observations, without reading target-scene Adobe matrices into the solver.
The sweep interpolation fixes the mild response, but makes B_strong worse.
No target-H lookup, deterministic >30deg identity veto, per-scene framing fit,
new branch threshold, or knowingly mismatched sigma was put in production.
