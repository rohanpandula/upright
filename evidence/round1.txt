1. ROOT CAUSE AND CONTROLLED EXPERIMENTS
--------------------------------------
This is a VP-stage family-selection bug exposed by the detector change.
The detector outputs 711 D_roll segments, independently of the solver seed.
The real vertical AND lateral horizontal families are present at seed 4.
The failure is not loss of the real lateral family or inability to detect it.

Ranked hypotheses tested after reproducing the exact public failure:
(1) A spurious VP displaces the real lateral VP in family selection.
(2) Assignment/refitting corrupts the true family's supports.
(3) Correct families reach the wrong optimizer basin solely from a bad start.

Seed-4 VPs in normalized image coordinates, with homogeneous third entry 1:
  vertical:     ( 0.649187,  4.352951), 452 supports, weight 409.264265
  real lateral: (15.146906, -1.322796), 157 supports, weight 248.585480
  spurious H:   ( 0.622890, -0.002252),  19 supports, weight  19.322961
The true vertical and lateral inlier arrays are EXACTLY the same at seeds
0 and 4: 452 and 157 supports. Their fitted VP positions differ slightly.
Hypothesis (2), corruption of those two support sets, is falsified here.

The approximate RANSAC estimator does emit extra models. In seed 4 its ninth
accepted hypothesis is near (0.628354,-0.007008). Its original vote mask has
179 supports, 163 vertical. Nearest-model reassignment leaves ten supports,
with a 5/5 orientation tie. The strict majority test picks horizontal on the
tie. TLS uses the five horizontal supports [499,501,585,633,652], whose lengths
are 25.52,25.12,20.81,18.37,17.51 px, then re-voting grows this to 19 supports.
The first four have roughly 20..28 degree slopes, the last roughly -4.4 deg
(modulo 180). This is a mixed accidental convergence, not the dominant wall
family. These facts explain how the distractor enters the candidate list;
they do not establish that each individual straight support is a false edge.
See round1_vp_candidates.json for the initial accepted hypotheses.

The decisive error is _base_solve's lateral selector:
  OLD: abs(vp_x) / hypot(vp_x, vp_y)
It measures from the image's TOP-LEFT origin, ignoring the principal point,
aspect ratio and focal geometry. It therefore gives the weak distractor
0.999993467 versus the real lateral family's 0.996208317. Family weight does
not protect the real family: it is overwritten by this sort. The distractor
becomes the X-axis family and the real lateral VP becomes the 'depth' VP in
the horizon energy. The starting Euler angles become approximately
(+4.387,+83.695,+27.080) deg; yaw is clipped to the optimizer's +68.755 deg
bound. The failed solution remains at that yaw bound. The robust picture-frame
loss suppresses large errors, allowing a finite, successful-looking result.

A better start escapes the catastrophic basin but still optimizes the wrong
family/horizon assignment and misses the requested accuracy. Initialization
is a consequence/amplifier, not an adequate mechanism-level repair.

The earlier rejected short-line trial is also reproducible: remove both proxy

  old selector: 4.551903 / 22.723167 deg
  new selector: 0.027708 /  0.019023 deg
That independent candidate-set perturbation exposes the same selector error.
Its detector policy is reproduced only in the evidence probe and regression;
it is not shipped as a detector change.

2. REPAIR
---------
_base_solve now sorts horizontal families by:
  abs(normalize(K0_inverse @ vp)[0])
This ranks directions in the source camera, around its principal point, with
pixel aspect/focal normalization and homogeneous handling of VPs at infinity.
For seed 4 the real lateral scores 0.993077509; the distractor scores 0.102057432.
The same corrected ordering supplies both initialization and family roles.
There is no fixture/seed name, retry, hardcoded pose or truth in production.

The production detector, NFA threshold, chains, refinement, RANSAC draws,
assignment/refitting, loss, optimizer and camera/mode constants are unchanged.
All four before/after detector arrays were checked exactly equal against
round1_original_segments.npz. All 80 freshly measured BEFORE scores exactly
match port_review_seeds.json. Historical review artifacts were preserved.

The new ordering also changes A_clean seed 6's secondary horizontal/horizon
choice; its primary lateral stays the same. That case changes from
0.012703/0.025548 to 0.016601/0.019042 deg. Other than A6 and D4, all 80 seed
scores are unchanged to 1e-8 deg. This is an intentional geometric ordering
change, not a seed-specific fallback.

3. QUALITY SIGNAL AND REJECTION POLICY
-------------------------------------
Before this fix, success checked structure availability and homography
finiteness. No fit-quality gate existed. The scalar robust cost is inadequate:
seed 0 costs 0.000330685; failed seed 4 costs only 0.001267882 (3.83x), despite
its catastrophic external error. Cauchy's compressed residual is not an angular
confidence measure.

New _alignment_quality checks the UNDAMPED fitted homography against each
selected observed family, without the Cauchy transformation:
  line' = line @ H_inverse
  d = normalize((-line'_y * width, line'_x * height))
  raw vertical residual = abs(d_x); horizontal residual = abs(d_y)
  m_v,m_h = medians weighted by SOURCE segment length within each family
  s = 4 / (max(width,height)-1)
  accept only finite medians with max(m_v,m_h) <= min(1,3*s)
The residuals are sine-angle errors in isotropic pixels. The source-length
median prevents many tiny chains from outweighing long structural supports;
it also tolerates minority outliers that the fit deliberately treats robustly.

Threshold justification: three times the existing four-pixel loss scale is
an explicit CHOICE, not a recovered Adobe rule. At 1400x1000 it is 0.491464 deg.
Among the 79 originally successful seed cases, the largest selected-family
weighted median is 0.127109 deg. After the fix, all 80 are <=0.127334 deg,
or <=0.777283*s. The four additional structure scenes have maxima <=0.162390
deg, or <=0.991275*s. Thus the gate leaves roughly 3x headroom over the largest
structure-control median without admitting the observed failure.

On the ORIGINAL BAD seed-4 fit and its ORIGINAL selected families, medians
are 2.707385 / 0.496885 deg, worst_scale=16.520512. The vertical signal is over
21x the worst originally successful seed median. It fails the check on its
own inputs, without a sibling solve or access to ground truth. The trial's
bad seed-9 fit also fails. Replaying the recorded bad theta through the
repaired public API rejects it in full/auto/level/vertical, with identity H,
reason='poor undamped family alignment', and quality.passed=False.

solve_ex exposes quality.family_median_deg, limit_deg, worst_scale, passed.
On rejection, rotation/VP diagnostics remain inspectable while H stays identity.
The gate runs before mode/override damping: intentional Auto/Level/Vertical
undercorrection must not be mistaken for a failed geometric solve. Tests check
both that distinction and rejection of a bad underlying fit in all four modes.
Guided keeps its separate guide-residual diagnostics; off requires no fit.

LIMIT: this is an internal alignment check, not a calibrated probability or
a bound on camera error. A wrong but internally consistent pair can still pass.
No truth-free check can guarantee every possible 20x ratio to a sibling's
external error, particularly when the sibling's error is arbitrarily near zero.
The evidence here establishes that the observed catastrophic solutions fail
by a wide margin. Majority-corrupted families and real-photo calibration remain
unverified. The 0.1-deg truth assertion belongs to the regression, not the gate.
