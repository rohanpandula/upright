FITTED kh model (n=16, maxerr 0.09 excluding the Y15 anomaly):
  kh = saturate( L0h * exp(-yaw^2/2 sigma_yaw^2) * (1 - exp(-pitch^2/2 sigma_gate^2)) )
  L0h=76.73  sigma_yaw=11.64 deg  sigma_gate=22.05 deg

KEY RESULT: sigma_yaw = 11.60 deg vs the paper's sigma_h = pi/15 = 12.00 deg (3.3% match),
and vs the float32 literal 1/(2 sigma_h^2)=11.3986332 compiled into CameraRaw.

CONCLUSION: Auto is gated on PITCH. With pitch present it corrects horizontals at
0.4-0.95; with pitch=0 (pure yaw) it is a no-op (0.001). A single theta = total tilt
cannot express this; the two weights key on different axes.
