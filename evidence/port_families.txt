METHOD / SCORING (same protocol as the Manhattan scenes)
  make_test_images.render_scene(family, rx, ry, rz, f), then
  upright_port.detect_segments -> solve_ex(detected, W, H, focal, mode).
  Truth = the family's own world lines projected through the generating
  rotation; only in-frame lines with >=40 px visible span are scored.
    vdev/hdev  median |angle from vertical/horizontal| of the known 3-D lines,
               deg (benchmark_accuracy.deviation)
    sim        max px distance between the port warp and ground_truth_H after
               the best similarity; isolates shape error
    frac       1 - sim / sim(identity), the fraction of the ideal correction
    fill       % of source pixels left inside the frame
    cand_*     for a refused cell, the same metrics on the undamped candidate
               the gate suppressed; cand_frac >= 0.5 means the refusal was false
  Modes auto/full everywhere, level additionally on roll. Fixture focal 1600 px
  at the family pose; extreme poses are run at 1600 px (fixture) and at 900 px
  (wide lens) because at 1600 px a 48-55 deg pitch leaves 0-5 visible lines.
  Solved f35 is quoted in the port's own diagonal convention (true 40.24 at
  1600 px, 22.63 at 900 px); focal was never re-estimated except where noted.
  Reference: identity residual is 98.4 px at 15/6, 566-7515 px at the extremes.

TASK 5 — WHICH FAMILIES FAIL, GEOMETRIC OR GATE
  No family fails on its own axis. Ranked by residual difficulty:
    CT_manhattan2   worst at extreme pitch, and the only scene that breaks
                    Full outright (pitch 40/900: frac -1.22). Geometric:
                    floor-plane family confusion, pitch 40 -> 52.5 deg.
                    Correctly refused at pitch 48/55 under the wide lens.
    NM_three_plane  same mechanism, milder (pitch 40/900: 47.0 deg, frac 0.83);
                    correctly refused at pitch 55.
    NM_railing      no break, but Full degrades at pitch 55/900 (frac 0.71,
                    sim 2183 px) and yaw 40/900 (0.94, 184.6 px).
    NM_single_wall  no break; Full 0.88-1.00 from pitch 40 to 55 and yaw 30-40.
    NM_curves       no break; best family. One false gate refusal.
  Mode is the dominant failure axis, not family: Auto is a near-no-op at pitch
  >=40 (every family, frac -0.13..-0.01, corpus-intended) and worse than identity
  at yaw 30 (-0.64..-0.43). Neither is a gate failure — ok=True and
  identity-like output; the yaw case is an objective/schedule failure
  (SIGMA_YAW 11.64 deg saturates by 30 deg).
  Gate census, real scenes: 20 refusals / 242 cells, all with identity output.
    18 correct  = 8 content starvation (pitch 48/55 at 1600 px, 0-5 visible
                  lines) + 10 where the suppressed candidate was worse than
                  identity (cand_frac -0.28..-2.74).
     2 false    = NM_curves yaw 35/900 px, auto and full: suppressed candidate
                  had cand_frac 0.965 (61.8 px against a 98.8 px identity
                  baseline) and would have been a good solve.
  Three false accepts: CT_manhattan2 pitch 40/900 px (full, frac -1.22) and the
  yaw-30 Auto solves on CT_manhattan2 and curves (frac -0.51, -0.64). The CT cell is
  passed because the relaxed bias test accepts any bound below the family's own
  scatter (bound 0.257 < median 0.649) even though the rotation is 12.5 deg
  wrong; the yaw-30 Auto cells are passed because their residuals stay under the
  scatter limit. The gate therefore no longer separates "self-consistent wrong
  family" from "good solve" on multi-plane scenes, and it cannot see an
  under-correcting Auto.
  Focal and fill failures are secondary and separate: calibration accepts an
  8-140% focal error at pitch 40-55, and default output framing leaves 0.0-7.5%
  of the source in frame at pitch >=40 (fixed by scale_output, not by the gate).
