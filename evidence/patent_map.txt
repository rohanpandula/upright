0. BOTTOM LINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The brief's two risk hypotheses are HALF RIGHT, and the correction matters:
   - "energy with adaptive weights" is NOT claimed anywhere.  The sigma values
     are paper Eq. 5 AND patent spec Eq. A5, but they appear in no claim.  Using
     them changes nothing about infringement.
   - "VP-selection ordering" IS covered, but not structurally.  No claim recites
     RANSAC, iteration counts, inlier thresholds or candidate ordering.  The
     ordering is covered only functionally, by 954 claim 1's "determining initial
     values for an energy function according to the extracted line segments".
2. The real risk is not any mechanism.  It is 460 claim 1 / 855 claim 1: a
   functional limitation ("homography ... computed at least in part according to
   one or more human perception-based criteria") with no structural hook.  Any
   auto-upright that damps the correction instead of fully rectifying reads on it.
3. Guided mode is the one mode with ZERO claim text over it.  The words "user",
   "guide" and "guide line" appear in none of the 100 claims of the five patents.
4. Two different intrinsics in H is claimed (460 c6/c13, 954 c5/c6) and is also
   published in paper Eq. 1-2.  Keep it: it is worth 32% of output error.
5. The Atlanta-world "additional orthogonal frame" is the one thing that is
   PATENTED-ONLY (885 c1/c12, 787 c1).  The shipped product only partially does
   it.  Dropping it entirely is both a design-around and a parity loss.

3. WHERE THE ACTUAL INFRINGEMENT RISK IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  ADAPTIVE WEIGHTS - risk LOW, and it is the wrong thing to worry about.
     The sigma values are not in any claim.  They are in paper Eq. 5:
       "lambda_v = exp(-psi^2 / 2 sigma_v^2) and lambda_h = exp(-theta^2 / 2 sigma_h^2) ...
        We fix them as sigma_v = pi/12 and sigma_h = pi/15 in our implementation."
     and in the spec of all five patents, identical, as Eq. A5:
       "the weights 'lambda_v' and 'lambda_h' may be adaptively determined using
        initial rotation angles ... sigma_v = pi/12 and sigma_h = pi/15."
     The shipped literals 0.2618f / 0.2094f (15.00 deg / 12.00 deg) are exactly
     those disclosed values, stored as 4-decimal authored floats
     (energy_function.txt §1).  So the clone is copying PUBLISHED numbers into an
     UNCLAIMED slot.
     CONSEQUENCE: swapping sigma, changing the decay shape, or dropping the
     weights altogether does not avoid any claim.  It does change output.
3.2  VP-SELECTION ORDERING - risk MEDIUM, and it is covered functionally only.
     No claim recites 2000 iterations, >=3 inliers, tau = sqrt(3), the 9-candidate
     cap, the count-ratio-then-residual-sum tournament, or the "detect -> cluster
     -> select -> solve" order.  All of that is unclaimed implementation detail.
     What is claimed is the role of the output:
       954 c1  "...determining initial values for an energy function according to
                the extracted line segments..."
       885 c1  "...identifying a first orthogonal frame ... and one or more
                additional orthogonal frames..."
       787 c1  "...identifying an orthogonal frame ... having an additional
                horizontal direction that shares a same vertical direction..."
     So a different RANSAC does not design around 954; a different *ordering of
     the VP families*, or dropping the second frame, does change 885/787 exposure.
3.3  THE REAL RISK - 460 c1 and 855 c1/c11, and it is functional.
     "the homography computed at least in part according to one or more human
     perception-based criteria"
     There is no structural hook: no named energy term, no named criterion, no
     detector, no solver.  Every dependent claim's mechanism is optional.  The
     claim is therefore read by *behaviour*: does the transform get computed
     under a criterion that trades full rectification against looking wrong?
     The product's Auto is exactly that (damped, pitch-gated, abstains at the
     noise floor).  Any clone that reproduces Auto's damping reproduces the
     limitation.  Swapping LSD for the bespoke detector, or Powell for
     Levenberg-Marquardt, is irrelevant to it.
3.4  WHY THE PRODUCT IS NOT ITSELF SAFE FROM 885/787 - and a clone need not be
     either.  The shipped product does not build a second orthogonal frame, yet
     it corrects non-Manhattan scenes.  So "we didn't implement Atlanta" is a
     weak non-infringement argument for 885/787, but it is an argument.
3.5  Everything in the family is independent-claim-functional.  There is no
     structural limitation anywhere in the five claim 1s.  This is why the
     "just reimplement the paper differently" instinct fails.

4. PUBLISHED vs PATENTED-ONLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  PUBLISHED (paper, citable prior art against third parties; NOT free, because
     Adobe also claimed the same subject matter with an earlier priority date).
     The paper was published June 2012; the provisional was 2012-04-27, so the
     paper does not invalidate the patents.  Published and re-embodied in spec:
       - H = K1 R K0^-1 with two DIFFERENT focal lengths (paper Eq. 1-2; the
         paper's "our reprojection model ... allows different focal lengths along
         x- and y-axes").  Re-claimed as 460 c6/c13, 954 c5/c6.
       - E_pic, E_eye, E_perk (Perkins Eq. 6), E_img (Jacobian), E_reg, E_focal.
         Re-claimed as 954 c2/c4.
       - Adaptive lambda_v/lambda_h with sigma_v = pi/12, sigma_h = pi/15.
         NOT re-claimed.  Spec only.
       - f == image width, cp == image centre, zero-rotation init.  Re-claimed as
         954 c17.
       - Joint MAP over K, R, M "in conjunction with each other".  Re-claimed as
         460 c9, 855 c6, 954 c1.
4.2  PATENTED-ONLY (no paper counterpart - genuinely new matter added in the
     applications, which is why they were continuations):
       - The Atlanta-world framing: "additional orthogonal frames having
         orthogonal directions that do not align" (885 c1/c12) and "an additional
         horizontal direction that shares a same vertical direction" (787 c1,
         c2/c10 "Atlanta world assumption").  The paper assumes a single Manhattan
         world throughout and never mentions a second horizontal VP.
       - 954 c6's "energy term describing a relationship between the x-axis focal
         length and the y-axis focal length" as a claim element (the paper has the
         term, so this is thin, but it is claimed).
       - 954 c17 as a packaged initialisation triple.
       - 855 c11's reprojection-free "planar transformation" claim - broader than
         anything the paper states.
4.3  PRODUCT-ONLY (neither paper nor claims - free to copy in the copyright sense,
     and irrelevant to patent exposure):
       - detector: gradient + a-contrario, 8 directions, +-22.5 deg, significance
         bins round(4*log8(maxdim)), 12 px / +-10 px merge tolerances
       - RANSAC 2000 / >=3 / tau = sqrt(3) / cap 9 / count-ratio 1.0 tournament
       - the 4-decimal float literals 0.2618f and 0.2094f (as authored values)
       - guided mode's 4 hard user segments
       - abstention-by-identity across all six slots
       - metadata-first DISCRETE focal switch, 35.0 fallback, clamp [10, 1000] on
         metadata only, no prior weight
       - degenerate retry, digest-gated recompute, UprightVersion ABI stamp
       - all six mode slots and the crop reset behaviour

5. MITIGATION - what actually reduces exposure while holding output
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranked by legal value per unit of parity lost.

5.1  GUIDED-FIRST.  Highest value.  "user", "guide" and "guide line" appear in
     ZERO of the 100 claims.  Guided takes 4 segments as hard constraints, solves
     s*K1*R*K0^-1, and never touches the energy model.  On its face it escapes
     954 c1 (no energy function), 460 c1 (no perceptual criterion), 787 c1 (no
     frame, no eye level) and 885 c1 (no frame).  Output is also *better*: guided
     reached 0.00 deg on both families at LOWER distortion than Full (1.476 vs
     1.486 p99 stretch) on the A_clean probe.
     RESIDUAL RISK: 855 c11.  "planar transformation ... computed at least in part
     according to one or more human perception based criteria" needs no
     reprojection and no energy.  A court can call user-drawn world edges
     "perception-based criteria".  Mitigate by never describing guided in
     perception terms in the docs, and by making the guided solve purely
     algebraic (constraint satisfaction, zero weights).
5.2  DROP THE EYE-LEVEL TERM.  Escapes 787 c1 outright (c1 requires criteria
     "that include the estimated eye-level"), and 460 c11 / 855 c8 / 885 c5.
     Parity cost is real: eye-level is what keeps a pitched shot from looking
     like a falling-building shot.  Partial mitigation: fold eye-level into the
     picture-frame term as a single horizon-angle residual, and do not expose a
     separate eye-level quantity.
5.3  DIFFERENT ENERGY FORMULATION, and do not use the four paper names.
     954 c2 lists "picture-frame alignment energy term, eye-level alignment energy
     term, perspective distortion energy term, and image distortion energy term".
     A formulation that is one combined residual over line-direction dispersion
     plus a single Jacobian-area penalty, and that never uses the four names, does
     not read on c2.  It still reads on 954 c1 (init from segments, then iterate).
     Realistic parity loss: moderate, and it is the honest way to differentiate.
5.4  NO ADAPTIVE WEIGHTS.  Zero legal value (§3.1) but two practical ones: it
     breaks the "same Eq. A5 as the spec" narrative a claim chart would like, and
     it is where the prototype is already known to be wrong (the ported L0 = 12.0
     over-corrects by ~40% against the fitted L0 ~ 3.5).  A single fitted damping
     curve is closer to output AND not the spec's curve.
5.5  DROP PERKINS.  Escapes 954 c4.  Cheap: E_perk only bites on cuboid corners
     and the product already has no measured arc protection (non_manhattan.txt:
     the arc residual never fitted, curves are the worst Auto family at 0.32-0.75
     deg post).  Parity cost near zero.
5.6  INITIALISE FOCAL FROM METADATA, NOT IMAGE WIDTH.  Escapes 954 c17 and the
     product already does it (35.0 fallback, clamp [10,1000] on metadata only).
     Free.
5.7  AVOID BUILDING A SECOND ORTHOGONAL FRAME.  Escapes 885 c1/c12 and 787 c1.
     Cluster VPs freely, cap the candidates, tournament-select, and pick one
     Manhattan triple.  This is what the product already does, so parity holds.
     Cost: worse on Atlanta-world scenes - which the product is already bad at.
5.8  KEEP TWO DIFFERENT INTRINSICS.  Do NOT design around 460 c6/c13 or 954 c5/c6.
     A shared K is off by ~32% on the homography; the two-intrinsic form is the
     single most load-bearing structural fact in the whole mechanism.
5.9  KEEP THE MODE SEMANTICS (Level = pure roll, Vertical zeroes pitch and yaw).
     855 c14 claims both.  Free to keep and free of parity cost.

WHAT DOES NOT WORK AS A DESIGN-AROUND:
  - swapping the line detector (LSD / EDLines / deep)      -> functional claims
  - swapping the optimiser (Powell / LM / Nelder-Mead)     -> functional claims
  - different sigma values, different weight shape         -> unclaimed anyway
  - different RANSAC iteration count or inlier threshold   -> unclaimed anyway
  - "we wrote it from the paper"                           -> the claims were
    drafted around the paper, with an earlier priority date

REALISTIC CONCLUSION: with 5.1-5.7 applied, a clone has a defensible
non-infringement position on 885, 787 and 954 c2/c4/c17, and a genuinely
arguable one on 460 c1 / 855 c1.  It does not have a clean position on 855 c11
unless guided is the *only* mode shipped.  Shipping Auto as well re-exposes
460/855.  There is no configuration in which this is free before 2033-04-26.
