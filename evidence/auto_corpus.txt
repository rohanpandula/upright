1. WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
Round 5 and Round 6 both attacked Auto over-correction, both improved raw distance to
Adobe on the strong scene, and both reverted with no change because the four-scene
shape metric regressed. All four cached scenes sit at 22 and 34 deg true pitch. The
corpus shows the port is nearly correct there and wrong everywhere else, so a
four-scene benchmark cannot see the defect. This round measures Adobe's own response
from 12,075 native transforms before touching anything.

2. PIPELINE
Per photo, report 3.1 two-camera model: rq(P H K0) = K1, R with K0 pinned to the
stored SOURCE camera (UprightFocalLength35mm, UprightCenterNormX/Y). Full is the
rectification reference, so the input pose is R0 = R_full^T and the input vertical
convergence c_in = conv(R0, K0) is DEFINED as the convergence Full removes exactly.
Auto's residual is conv(R_auto R0, K1_auto). removed = 1 - residual / input.
conv() is the port's own P95-P5 vertical-direction statistic on a 61x41 frame grid.
No line detector, no image decode: the argument is geometric, the transforms are real.

7. THE SHIPPED CHANGE
Auto's vertical response is now a function of UNDAMPED CAMERA PITCH, not of the
detected keystone spread. The reason, in numbers: detected keystone compresses with
pitch nonlinearly (scene A 22.0 deg true pitch -> 12.3 deg detected; scene B 34.4 ->
16.7), so a curve written in keystone units cannot express a curve measured in pitch
units. Round 6's "camera units" objection was to swapping units while keeping the same
monotone Gaussian; the corpus curve is not that.

  AUTO_PITCH_KNOTS_DEG      = 1 3 5 7 9 11 13.5 16.5 20 24.5 31 40.5
  AUTO_PITCH_KNOTS_FRACTION = .532 .631 .741 .815 .789 .790 .692 .589 .561 .468 .170 0
  AUTO_PITCH_GAIN           = 1.20
  correction_fraction_pitch(pitch_rad) = clamp(gain * interp(|pitch|), 0, 1)

Piecewise-linear through the corpus bin medians, clamped. _auto_pitch_scale and the
fraction-to-scale inversion are UNCHANGED: the detected keystone still sets the
inversion target, only the response argument moved. correction_fraction_vertical and
L0_V/SIGMA_V are kept as the superseded keystone-units form.
Gain 1.20 is the only calibrated scalar: the four cached scenes sit above the corpus
median at their pitch, and 1.20 is the gain at which scene A reproduces Adobe's own
measured 0.652. Sensitivity: 1.16 -> agg raw 27.147 / shape 0.6909; 1.20 -> 27.764 /
0.6783; 1.30 -> 29.549 / 0.708. 1.20 also holds A and C shape at parity.

9. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE
Established: Adobe's Auto response is hump-shaped in pitch, peaks ~0.80 near 6-12 deg,
and is near zero beyond 35 deg; the shipped monotone 13.33 deg Gaussian is wrong at both
ends; the four-scene benchmark is blind to it because all four scenes sit at 22/34 deg.
Not established: the mixture weights (a 30% no-op branch survives at 27-35 deg); whether
the argument is camera pitch or an internal rotation difference (round 6 section 3 still
stands - this measures the response, not the implementation stage); whether the +0.58
D-roll raw regression is acceptable long term, since D is the only rolled scene and the
corpus curve ignores roll coupling.
