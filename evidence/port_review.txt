1. WHAT I AM LEAST CONFIDENT IN, RANKED
======================================
Ranking is likelihood of a wrong generalization or an unrecovered algorithm,
not just whether a constant's bytes were read correctly. None is a probability
estimate. "Overturn" means evidence that would falsify the current choice or
its claimed scope. Existing VP/solver weaknesses are distinguished from changes.

1) HIGHEST: reconstructed detector supports and their interaction with the
   unchanged VP estimator. Connected components, TLS halving, Sobel/NMS,
   percentile threshold, sigma=1.2 blur, subpixel interpolation, traversal and
   1200px working policy are our algorithms/policies, not recovered helpers.
   Shorter/more numerous chains also change the randomized VP search.
   Concrete counterexample: D_roll seed 4 gives 5.626297 / 22.713280 degrees
   with ok=True. With the frozen OLD detector segments and the same NEW energy,
   that seed gives 0.052250 / 0.105294. Default new-detector seed 0 instead gives
   0.013845 / 0.000620. These are not all equivalent detector improvements.
   Overturn: oracle segment outputs, or new raster families / seed or ordering
   changes that substantially worsen accuracy. The seed counterexample already
   overturns any blanket claim of reliable improvement.
   Cheap check: run seeds 0..9 on cached D_roll detections; see the seed repro
   below. Also rotate/transpose the short-line tests and vary contrast/spacing.

2) HIGH: Cauchy and s=4/(maxdim-1). The literal inspired the scale, but neither
   the Cauchy loss nor its use of that literal was recovered. At M=1400 this is
   about 0.164 degrees of sine-angle residual. Resolution changes the balance
   between picture-frame errors, horizon error and the destination-focal term.
   Cauchy's influence shrinks on large errors: a misclassified family can get
   ignored rather than corrected. The original f32 camera tolerance does not
   prove any of these objective semantics.
   Overturn: held-out images where this scale rejects true structural lines,
   a scale/loss change destroys the gain, or a traced native evaluator disagrees.
   Cheap check: the full 0.5x/1x/2x Cauchy/Huber and least-squares experiment in
   section 3. Also resize one raster and focal together while keeping its pose.
   Result: the exact 92% does not survive least squares, but a large gain does.

3) HIGH: mean-normalized source lengths IN THE ENERGY. Recovered detector
   weights do not prove their transfer into this objective. My old PROVEN
   annotation was too strong; the code and README now say this is our choice.
   Algebra: (1/N) * (length_i/mean_all_lengths) = length_i/sum_all_lengths.
   The denominator includes depth lines, outliers and other unused segments.
   Adding unassigned clutter can therefore reduce E_pic relative to E_eye/E_f
   without adding a single scored structural constraint. Long bad lines also
   get extra weight. This is not harmless normalization.
   Overturn: objective stability changes when irrelevant supports are appended,
   or uniform/assigned-family normalization wins on held-out scenes.
   Cheap check: freeze families and evaluate the same theta after doubling the
   total length with unassigned supports; E_pic halves, the other terms do not.
   Uniform weights and 0.5x/2x normalizers are measured in section 3.

4) MEDIUM-HIGH: removing the pseudo-Perkins term improves an objective by
   deleting a wrong term, but leaves genuine three-ray junctions unconstrained.
   A two-line crossing is not a fork; that domain correction is sound. It does
   not establish that omitting the real inequality/barrier is safe in photos.
   E_img is also absent, so low axis error says nothing about distorted curves.
   Overturn: a real fork with a rectified angle <=90 degrees, or severe shape
   distortion despite small axis residuals.
   Cheap check: construct one labeled three-ray fork and measure all three
   output angles, plus circle/conic aspect on a curved fixture. There is no
   port-level fork/shape oracle today. The extra structure probe below measures
   straight-line axes only, not circle preservation.

5) MEDIUM-HIGH: the new unsmoothed FALLBACK is a clean-room repair, not a native
   stage. It preserves the previous smoothed pass and only runs if it is empty.
   That avoids changing large-scene candidates, but can miss a short line in an
   image where an unrelated long line is already detected. Each pass uses the
   recovered NFA<1 rule; the combined nominal false-alarm budget is <=2, NOT 1.
   Independence/calibration of preselected, correlated Sobel supports is itself
   unproven. Exact binomial arithmetic is not a detector calibration proof.
   Overturn: unacceptable extra false detections, mixed-content short-line loss,
   or an oracle showing smoothing should have been handled differently.
   Cheap check: 30 fixed noise seeds at four sizes (results below), and a 32x32
   image with a long line at y=8 plus the short line at y=24. Only the long line
   survives in that mixed image. This limitation is explicit, not solved here.

6) MEDIUM: refinement/merge reconstruction. Float32 1.0 and perpendicular 6px
   correct earlier interpretations, but treating argument 10 as a support gap,
   halving bent TLS supports, longest-first merge order and Euclidean projected
   intervals remain guesses. A native signature is not the native algorithm.
   Overturn: parallel nearby structures or bent/rasterized supports whose
   grouping differs from native outputs, or order-dependent missing segments.
   Cheap check: the merge strict-boundary/unsorted-gap/bend tests, then reverse
   input order and sweep line separation 5.9/6/6.1 px. Exact native ordering
   cannot be checked without a detector oracle.

7) LOWER: observed H^-T support lines instead of VP-through-midpoint virtual
   lines. This follows the paper's picture-frame geometry and avoids erasing
   within-family disagreement. Native evaluator equivalence remains unproven.
   Overturn: a recovered native evaluator explicitly scoring virtual supports,
   or independently labeled line families consistently scoring worse this way.
   Cheap check: supply two imperfectly convergent lines sharing one fitted VP;
   observed-line errors must retain their disagreement. Frozen OLD segments
   isolate this whole energy change from detector changes (section 3).

8) LOWEST mathematical risk: pixel-angle conversion for E_pic, log-space
   binomial-tail arithmetic, corrected 1.0f/6px call constants, and the quadratic
   interpolation formula. Aspect-ratio invariance and independent small-tail
   sums test the algebra. Their insertion into Adobe's pipeline is not proven.
   Overturn: an aspect-ratio round trip changes E_pic, an independent tail sum
   disagrees, or raw call-site evidence contradicts detector_corrections.txt.
   Cheap check: existing pixel-angle/binomial tests and the scalar-width audit
   commands in detector_corrections.txt. This review relies on that prior byte
   audit; it did not re-disassemble the binary or run Adobe.

Two additional confirmed weaknesses in the current energy path:
* The pixel-angle fix is INCOMPLETE: E_eye still normalizes [-l_y,l_x] directly
  in normalized coordinates. A physical 45-degree horizon gives squared
  residual 0.8 at 1000x500 and 0.2 at 500x1000; a pixel-angle term gives 0.5 in
  both. Cheap check: cross([0,0,1],[500/w,500/h,1]), then use the exact horizon
  direction expression at upright_port.py:_energy_residuals. This is unchanged
  horizon code, but contradicts a blanket statement that the energy now uses
  pixel angles throughout. It was not repaired as part of this detector fix.
* VP classification also mixes conventions: estimate_vanishing_points uses
  pixel dx/dy, _classify uses normalized dx/dy. A 100x80px direction at 1400x1000
  changes from horizontal to vertical under the second rule. Families near the
  divider, large rolls and unusual aspect ratios remain vulnerable. This
  predates this review's changes and is not covered by the five-scene metric.

2. SHORT-LINE FAILURE: REPRODUCTION, CAUSE, REPAIR
=================================================
Original public call returned: 12->1, 16->1, 20->0, 24->0, 32->0.
The regression was written first and failed at 20/24/32, both orientations.
Instrumentation inspected the chain, refinement and NFA boundaries separately.

  canvas M  nearest bins  smoothed n=k  NFA=M^4/8^6  raw n=k  raw NFA
  12        5             6             0.079102      8        0.001236
  16        5             6             0.250000      8        0.003906
  20        6             6             0.610352      8        0.009537
  24        6             6             1.265625      8        0.019775
  32        7             6             4.000000      8        0.062500

The 8 raster samples x=3..10 produce only six aligned smoothed samples x=4..9
on each edge. Gaussian smoothing rounds the line ends enough to fail angular
alignment there. At M=20, strict len>round(4log(M)/ln8) drops these six samples
BEFORE NFA, although NFA<1. At M=24/32, removing only that filter does NOT fix
the failure: six samples genuinely fail the M^4 NFA. Disabling only NFA also
does not fix the original because the bin gate runs first. A floor bin formula
would fix 20, still not 24/32. A maxdim-scaled geometric tolerance is not the
cause; the only such VP gate occurs downstream and detect_segments never calls
it. _chain_points' 3-sample representation minimum is not hit in this repro.

Final repair:
* Extract the existing smoothed pass into _detect_gradient_segments, retaining
  its nearest-bin gate, preprocessing and NFA<1 criterion exactly.
* If that pass returns nothing, run it without Gaussian blur and without the
  rounded-bin proxy. Those eight real support samples pass the same exact NFA.
* Merge and rescale exactly as before. No special casing of canvas dimensions,
  line orientation, or the five fixture names; no NFA bypass or invented count.
* This adds a possible second search, so nominal combined expected false alarms
  are bounded by 2 only if each pass's null-model assumptions hold. The raw
  fallback and this budget are explicit additional divergences from native.

Final public repro: 12->1, 16->1, 20->1, 24->1, 32->1. At M=64 the same short
line is still rejected: even eight perfect samples have NFA=1. This is a proper
negative control, not a guarantee that a fixed short line survives all padding.
The fallback is deliberately limited to empty detections; see rank 5.

Why the repair was narrowed:
Removing both bin filters globally and doubling the NFA test count changed only
eight D_roll supports, yet seed 9 went to 4.551903 / 22.723167 degrees, ok=True.
Tightening even the unchanged smoothed pass's NFA budget also changed candidates
and broke accuracy. Those attempts were rejected, not shipped. The final first
pass produces arrays identical to the pre-review detector on the bundled
scenes. The rejected trial is preserved in port_review_trial_seed_comparison.json
solely to substantiate detector/VP sensitivity. Preserving the old candidate
policy is a compatibility decision, not a claim that its bin proxy is exact.

Added regressions (original 47 tests retained):
* short line at all five requested sizes, horizontal and transposed vertical;
* 64px negative NFA control;
* D_roll at seeds 0 and 9, to reject the regression seen during the repair.
Result: 50 tests pass. The broader seed sweep's existing seed-4 failure remains
OPEN. There is no passing regression assertion pretending that case is fixed.

3. LOSS / SCALE / LENGTH SENSITIVITY
====================================
Protocol: Full mode, seed=0, default 1200px detection working side, true focal.
Five original rasters are never regenerated. Cache detections BEFORE swapping
the loss; keep VP estimation, starting point, mode weights, camera parameters,
optimizer and evaluation lines unchanged. The evaluator uses known wall-A
geometry independently of detector inliers: two extreme verticals and two
extreme lateral horizontals per scene. It does not score depth, crop or shape.

r = dot(pixel-normalized transformed observed direction, perpendicular axis).
Cauchy rho = s^2*log(1+(r/s)^2).
Huber rho = r^2 for |r|<=s, otherwise 2*s*|r|-s^2.
Least squares rho = r^2; scale has no effect, so do not fabricate three LS rows.
The solver receives sign(r)*sqrt(rho), preserving small-error curvature.
s = factor*4/(max(w,h)-1); both robust losses use the SAME scale convention.

The re-expressed default residual is checked against the implementation. The
pre-review default outputs are also checked against the saved historical JSON.
Final review and pre-review detector outputs/scores coincide on all five scenes.
Thus the table tests the ORIGINAL 92% claim as well as the delivered repair.

Full table and normalization controls follow in the generated numeric appendix.

Conclusion: the gain is not a narrow optimum at exactly s=4/(M-1) or uniquely
Cauchy. Huber at 0.5s is slightly better on this metric; no new default was
selected from that result. Robust variants retain 89.1-92.3% reduction. Plain
least squares retains 80.2%, so the precise 92% is NOT loss-independent. Uniform
length weights retain 88.5%; scaling the mean denominator 0.5x/2x gives
92.7%/91.8%. On OLD detector segments, the same loss variants retain only
42.6-65.7% improvement: detector/VP changes materially contribute to the result.
Sensitivity on these same fixtures cannot prove absence of overfitting.

Generalization checks beyond this loss table:
* All five main scenes are synthetic. A/C/D share a pose or scene; B is a
  stronger pose of the same corner; 01_input is another synthetic corner.
  These are not five independent photographs. Knowing the true focal removes
  a major real-world source of error.
* Wrong-focal legacy diagnostic is kept in the JSON: 01_input horizontal error
  goes from 0.223640 to 0.270213 degrees at 1200px focal, despite the headline
  improvement measured with its correct 1400px focal. It gets worse.
* Existing non-Manhattan research fixtures give some structural variety but
  are still synthetic and share a 15deg/6deg pose and renderer. Their independent
  straight-line scores are listed below. They are not a new real-photo holdout,
  nor a measurement of curved-feature preservation.
* Seed sweep: A/B/C seeds 0..9, D_roll seeds 0..49. D_roll seed 4 is catastrophic
  and still reports ok=True; every other tested D_roll seed stays below 0.1deg
  in both families. This one observed failure is not a statistical rate claim.

4. WHAT THE FROZEN REPORT MUST DISTINGUISH OR CORRECT
====================================================
The report literally names upright_matched.py in section 3.6. That artifact was
not edited by this task. Its historical measurements are not retroactively
false because port/upright_port.py changed. They must not be relabeled as tests
of this port. The following is the complete detector/energy discrepancy ledger
from the named sections, including inherited omissions that prevent fidelity.
This ledger separates prior byte-audit corrections from our implementation
choices. Values/addresses come from detector_corrections.txt unless noted.

Section 3.5e, detector:
D1. "No binomial NFA" is contradicted by the earlier byte audit. The port uses
    M^4*P(Binomial(n,1/8)>=k), in log space. +0x34=.125 is a null probability,
    not interpolation. Log-space computation is ours; native integer arithmetic
    and any overflow behavior are not intentionally reproduced.
D2. "round(...), 16 at 4000" is a report/native-byte conflict. ROUNDSS imm=9
    floors: native count=15 at 4000. The port retains nearest=16 for the original
    acceptance tests. It uses strict len>bins in the SMOOTHED pass only; the
    new raw fallback uses NFA directly. It does not reproduce native length-bin
    histograms/traversal. A bin count alone is not the full a-contrario test.
D3. "(obj,10,1/2048)" is a float-width error. The loaded float32 is 1.0; the
    port uses distance=1px. Reading adjacent floats as one double caused the
    earlier 1/2048 interpretation. The port uses 10 as a gap bound, not ten
    proved native inner iterations, and 99 as a cap on its own TLS split passes.
D4. "12px perpendicular and +/-10 along-gap" is wrong according to the branch
    audit. The port uses <=6px perpendicular, strict start>-10/end<+10 interval
    containment, and does not extend the survivor or bridge arbitrary gaps.
    Longest-first order and Euclidean intervals are independently chosen.
D5. Native gradient preprocessing and exact support tracing were never recovered
    in full. Our sigma=1.2 Gaussian, 85th percentile, Sobel, four-way NMS,
    eight-connected components, quadratic subpixel shift clipped to half a pixel,
    and recursive TLS halving are choices. Eight scans/cos22.5 are shared facts,
    not evidence for all these other steps. The new raw fallback is also ours.
D6. Mean-normalized lengths were observed in a detector weight block. Their use
    as source-length weights in E_pic, and normalization over ALL source lines,
    are our choices. The previous PROVEN energy annotation was an overclaim.
D7. The camera tolerance is (4/(M-1))^2, not (4/M)^2. Other tolerance literals
    belong to a separate camera block, not invented detector gates. Assigning
    its four-pixel scale to a Cauchy loss is not a recovered native operation.
D8. The port has a default 1200px working side and no calibration stage, native
    coarse curve payload/pass B, estimate_MKR sequence, opt_H5 barrier stage or
    handle_degen_case retry pipeline. Those named report stages are not present
    just because the public API returns a homography.

Section 3.5e, VP (unchanged approximate implementation, not newly recovered):
D9. Native is explicitly NOT pair RANSAC in the report. Port samples segment
    pairs, uses 2000 as a trial budget and further caps candidates at 1200.
    It does not implement the described native per-segment candidate mechanism.
D10. Native sqrt(3) is a fixed vote clamp, not a binary inlier threshold. Port
     treats it as an inlier gate, floors it at .002*maxdim, and even uses the
     unscaled TAU at initial reassignment before later refitting with scaled tau.
D11. Port uses fresh-inlier coverage, Jaccard>0.6 deduplication, majority pixel
     orientation filtering, TLS refits and weight sorting. This is not the
     native count-ratio/residual tie-break/tournament stage or exact vote-set
     deduplication described by sections 3.5e/3.5g.
D12. Native two-VP/margin/degeneracy logic and one-VP horizon-only retry are not
     implemented. Port requires a classified vertical AND horizontal family,
     uses heuristic lateral-family ordering, otherwise returns identity/failure.

Section 3.6, objective / solver / evaluation:
E1. Reference artifact: section names upright_matched.py (scipy prototype);
    this task changes the separate numpy/OpenCV MIT port. A round trip in the
    two-camera algebra does not establish matching detectors or energies.
E2. E_pic now transforms actual observed supports by H^-T. The previous port
    scored VP-through-midpoint virtual lines, hiding within-family scatter.
E3. E_pic directions now use pixel angles, not normalized x/y angles. E_eye
    still uses normalized angles: do not describe this as a complete correction
    of all objective geometry. Only one selected lateral horizontal family
    receives picture-frame alignment; depth contributes through the horizon.
E4. Cauchy replaces squared picture-frame error. Its resolution scale and its
    insertion point are our decisions; neither is a recovered Adobe loss.
E5. Source lengths divided by their global mean and the total segment count
    replace equal chain weighting. Priors/horizon are not normalized the same
    way. This changes their relative strength when support composition changes.
E6. The arbitrary two-line-intersection pseudo-Perkins penalty was removed.
    No detected three-ray forks, Perkins inequality, log barrier or interior-point
    optimization replaces it. E_img/curved-edge anisotropy is also absent.
E7. Destination focal residual (f1/f0-1)^2 remains, with SOURCE K0 fixed. This
    is distinct from a recovered source-focal selector/prior. Metadata/geometry
    source switching, source-center estimation and native camera fitting are
    not implemented here. The free K1 center/focal cannot fix every wrong K0.
E8. Mode-specific scalar weights and adaptive schedules are chosen approximations
    and are still used INSIDE separate per-mode solves, then rotation damping
    is applied. Do not infer a shared cached full solve across modes from the
    section 3.6 description (or the stronger measurement in section 3.5h).
    Existing Auto SIGMA_V=14.58deg/L0_V=3.56 also differ from matched.py's
    13.33deg/2.64; pitch-versus-keystone units remain unresolved. This review did
    not tune damping or claim to resolve the units.
E9. Bounded finite-difference Levenberg-Marquardt is our optimizer. No native
    constrained evaluator/gradient, barrier, retry logic or convergence/status
    test is recovered. ok=True means a finite homography was returned, not
    reliable correction, as D_roll seed 4 demonstrates.
E10. The section's 0.49/0.38, 0.96/0.31 etc. are older matched.py convergence
     scores/crop measurements. The new 92.3% uses mean absolute AXIS scores
     from independent truth, eight family medians on four TIFFs; the fifth scene
     makes the reduction 90.4%. These quantities are not interchangeable.
     There is no new Adobe solve or crop/shape comparison supporting "matches
     Adobe within .25deg" for this port. Greater rectification accuracy than
     Adobe on synthetic truth would not itself establish Adobe fidelity.
E11. "Fixes all three defects" and a single fixed % Auto overcorrection cannot
     be carried over to this port. Missing objectives, VP failures, focal error,
     crop/output policy, seed variation and the wrong-focal counterexample
     remain. Historical measured tables should keep their actual artifact and
     protocol; new port claims need separate tables and the above limitations.

5. HONEST BOTTOM LINE
======================
This port is a good clean-room approximation with known divergences. It has
correct two-camera algebra and useful default-seed accuracy on a narrow synthetic
benchmark, but its detector, VP selection, robust objective, normalization,
fallback and optimizer are independently chosen algorithms, and major native
constraints/stages are absent. A catastrophic seed-dependent solve and confirmed
aspect-ratio errors rule out calling it a faithful reimplementation. The robust
gain survives reasonable loss/scale perturbations on these fixtures; that is
evidence of local usefulness, not evidence of Adobe equivalence or broad accuracy.
