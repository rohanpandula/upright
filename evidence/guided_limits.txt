1. HEADLINE — GUIDED REQUIRES count == 4 AND EXACTLY 4 SEGMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Two failing shapes were captured, and they fail in DIFFERENT ways.

(a) THREE SEGMENTS SUPPLIED, count=4  (case f3_good)
    RESULT|f3_good|clicked=true| all six UprightTransform_* = nil;
    UprightFocalLength35mm=35; PerspectiveUpright=5; UprightTransformCount=6;
    UprightFourSegmentsCount=4; UprightVersion=151388160
    → NOTHING was written. No slot, no focal. The Update click landed
      (clicked=true) but the solve produced no transform at all.
    → Partial sets do not fall back; they silently produce nothing.

(b) FOUR SEGMENTS SUPPLIED BUT count=8  (case p8_good)
    RESULT|p8_good|clicked=true| slot 5 = IDENTITY (1,0,0,0,1,0,0,0,1);
    slots 1-4 carry a REAL unguided solve (max|H-I| 0.194 on slot 4);
    focal = 39.8008; UprightFourSegmentsCount = 0.
    → Guided did not run. The supplied guides were DISCARDED (count reset to 0)
      and the host fell back to the ordinary Full/Level/Vertical/Auto path,
      which then wrote slots 1-4 and a focal.
    → So a count mismatch degrades to "unguided", it does not error.

Interpretation: `UprightFourSegmentsCount` is the authority for how many
segments to read, and the solver is compiled for exactly four. Supplying 4
with count=8 makes it read 8 slots, find 4 empty, and abandon the guided
path; supplying 3 with count=4 makes it read four slots, find one empty, and
produce no homography. The silent-discard behaviour in (b) is the dangerous
one: it looks like success (a focal was written, slots are populated) unless
you specifically check slot 5.

Practical rule for any port or harness: assert count == 4 AND all four
segment keys non-empty, and verify slot 5 is non-identity afterwards. A
successful-looking run with an identity slot 5 means guided did not execute.

Practical consequences
  * A guided success is not self-evidently correct. c2 returns success, writes
    a plausible-looking matrix and zero residual on the guides, yet corrects
    only 0.082 where the scene needs 0.215. Residual-on-guides is a useless
    quality signal for degenerate input; check max|H-I| against a normal
    reference too.
  * One contradictory guide is enough to lose the whole solve. Do not expect
    robustness to a single bad line.
  * Slot 5 absent (nil) — not identity — is the marker of a guided solve that
    ran and produced nothing. An identity slot 5 means the solve never ran
    (§1b); nil means it ran and failed.
  * Guided overwrites slots 1-4. Capture any prior Auto/Full result before
    invoking guided if it is wanted.

Harness notes from this run
